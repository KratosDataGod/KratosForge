# Govern a workspace: who can endorse, and where labels end up

The Sales lakehouse is about to be shared across the company. Governance asked the team to endorse it properly and raise its label to Confidential, and wants to know beforehand exactly which items will carry which label.

## What you'll do

Work out twelve governance outcomes: who may promote, certify or mark Master data, and which sensitivity label each item and export ends up with after a label change.

## How it runs here

This lab is a model, not Fabric. decisions.json holds each scenario and the decision you record for it. Check my work reads it as data; Run lists every value so you can see what the checks read.

## What this run proves

- Your answers follow the endorsement overview (who can promote, certify and mark Master data, and which items can't be endorsed) and the information protection and downstream inheritance pages (autogenerated items, create-from inheritance, manual and more restrictive labels kept, export paths), checked against a model on this PC.

## What it doesn't prove

- That any label or badge is applied: nothing runs here and no label policy is read.
- Fully automated downstream inheritance, inheritance from data sources and default or mandatory labeling, which this exercise leaves out.
- Label protection settings such as encryption, and what a label does to access in other tenants.
