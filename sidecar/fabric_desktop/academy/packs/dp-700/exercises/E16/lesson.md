# Sensitivity labels and endorsement: promote, certify, label inheritance

Governance wants trusted lakehouses marked and confidential data labelled from source to report. The team needs to know who may do what, and where labels end up on their own.

## What you'll do

Choose who can promote and certify items, and predict how a sensitivity label flows to the items built from a labelled one.

## Notes

- Promoted: anyone with write permission on the item. Certified and Master data: only users a Fabric administrator specifies.
- A label on an item propagates downstream to dependent items; this is on by default.
- Items a lakehouse or warehouse generates take its label.
- To apply labels, a user must be allowed to and the label must be published to them.

## What this run proves

- You can pick the right feature, setting or operator for each scenario, with the reason and source shown afterwards.

## What it doesn't prove

- That you can do it in Fabric: nothing is set up or run here. Doing it on a real item is FABRIC VERIFIED work.
